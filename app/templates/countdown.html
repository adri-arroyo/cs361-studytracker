HOW TO USE


Do you have an important project that's due soon? Or maybe finals are coming up and you want to know how many days you have left to study?

Calculate how many days, weeks or months are left until a given FUTURE date. Make sure you complete all the required fields on the page. Click the Submit button once you are finished.

The "Combo" option will return a string with how many months, weeks and days are left. This is best used when your future date is more than three months away.
